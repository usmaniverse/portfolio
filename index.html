JeenyRide-hailing · Saudi Arabia & Jordan

Rebuilding a ride-hailing app without stopping it

Jeeny's rider and driver apps ran on a legacy Objective-C/MVC codebase, in two markets, at about a million ride requests a day. Every feature was slow to build, and the business couldn't pause releases for a rewrite.

The hard call

No big-bang rewrite. We moved to Swift and MVVM one module at a time, so every release still went out. New features went straight to SwiftUI, and older UIKit screens were bridged in, then rewritten where it paid off.

The modules became a platform: a shared UI kit with Figma design tokens, and map, payment and networking modules reused by both apps. I then took on delivery for the whole squad across iOS, Android, backend and QA, and grew the iOS team from 5 to 15.

~30% faster feature builds−35% P1/P2 incidents

Jeeny today: rated 4.7 on the App Store from about 291K ratings.

Zameen & BayutProperty portals · Pakistan & UAE

Zameen.com and Bayut.com: two portals, one modern codebase

Zameen and Bayut are leading property portals in Pakistan and the UAE. Both iOS apps were Objective-C monoliths, and teams kept blocking each other on every release.

The hard call

Split the monolith before redesigning anything. We moved both apps to Swift and VIP Clean Architecture with CocoaPods modules, so teams could ship their areas independently.

With that in place, I led the redesign of both apps to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines with Product and Design, and mentored junior engineers through the move to Swift.
